# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
- **School staff** — teachers / UKS (Usaha Kesehatan Sekolah) coordinators who record iron-supplement (tablet tambah darah) intake for adolescent girls.
- **Puskesmas / health workers** — manage consolidated health data across schools, monitor anemia.
- **Government health institutions** — North Lombok Kabupaten health department; endorse and rely on the program.
- **Parents / adolescent girls** — the indirect beneficiaries of a healthier, better-monitored population.

The landing page is the public face of the program. Its primary job is to establish credibility and drive Android app downloads.

## Product Purpose

Gibran Persebaya is a school-based recording and reporting app for iron/blood-tablet supplements (`tablet tambah darah`) given to adolescent girls, to prevent and monitor anemia. The landing page exists to explain what the program does, why it matters, who it is endorsed by, and to get visitors to download the app.

## Brand Personality

Trusted and authoritative. Official, calm, reassuring. Three words: **calm · credible · civic**. This is a government-backed health program, not a consumer flipper — the page should feel institutional in the best way: confident, warm-yet-official, human.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream/beige scaffold with template-feel and AI-slop pattern reveals (identical card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, hero-metric clichés, gradient text, glassmorphism-by-default).
- Cold clinical hospital-white corporate look — this still has to feel human and community-minded.
- Saturated "developer-tool" dark mode.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence of trust first** — institution logos, clear official framing, precise copy; the program's authority is its differentiator.
- **Show, don't tell** — demonstrate the actual recording/monitoring workflow visually rather than describing it with stacked cards.
- **Calm focus** — one primary conversion path (download) per viewport; no competing noise.
- **Human-scale, official tone** — warm enough to feel like a community program, structured enough to feel sanctioned.
- **Accessibility by default** — WCAG AA contrast, keyboard/screen-reader support, reduced-motion handled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1; visible focus states; semantic landmarks and ARIA where needed.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: all animation has a reduced/crossfade fallback.
- Indonesian-first content; clear, plain language for a broad non-technical audience.
